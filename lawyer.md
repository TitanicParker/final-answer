# The Case Between Lawyers

## The sustainable formulation

The strongest case is not that the 2017 Parkinsonian diagnosis was fabricated or wholly wrong. Later genetics makes that position untenable. The sustainable case is that a genuine Parkinsonian syndrome was identified within a broader presentation whose central foot/gait relationship remained unresolved, and that the record may have become more settled than the clinical encounter actually was.

The proof architecture should therefore proceed in sequence rather than by conclusion.

### 1. Establish the originating state

The referral and admission records support a combined presentation involving feet, gait, toe-clawing, tone, movement initiation and function. Neurology documented strong Parkinsonism, dystonic-looking gait, structural foot abnormalities and an unresolved relationship between the foot/leg symptoms and the neurological syndrome.

A particularly important documentary omission is analytical rather than factual: the clinician recorded longstanding foot treatment and the reduction in foot pain when occupational weight-bearing reduced, yet the surviving reasoning does not visibly establish how the painful feet affected the gait being interpreted neurologically, or how the neurological disorder affected the feet.

That is the missing integrative question.

### 2. Prove the Protest before building on it

The patient later says he protested before discharge that the feet remained unresolved and used the NCT / bald-tyres analogy. Treat that as positive patient evidence, not as a contemporaneous institutional fact.

Required proof includes timing, hearers, any witnesses, any teaching/ward metadata, authorial knowledge and consistency with surrounding conduct.

The defence point that the patient accepted Parkinsonism does not dispose of the Protest. The relevant distinction is between accepting the Parkinsonian diagnosis/treatment rationale and accepting that the feet had been adequately explained or disposed of.

### 3. Separate omission from motive

If the Protest is established, identify whether the completed record omitted it and whether the omission was material. Do not infer intent merely from absence.

The first legally and evidentially significant proposition is not "concealment." It is that a material disagreement may have failed to enter the durable synthesis.

Only after that is established should purpose or state of mind be examined.

### 4. Treat downstream records as a dependency problem

Do not argue that every later clinician shared any originating knowledge or motive. That is unnecessary and vulnerable.

Instead ask, document by document, which propositions were independently observed and which were inherited. Later repetition may prove that the representation propagated; it does not by itself prove that the originating issue was independently re-tested.

This is especially important for complaint and governance outputs. A later process can be procedurally genuine and still depend on an inherited historical information state.

### 5. Use treatment-response evidence carefully

Procyclidine and levodopa-related observations matter because the feet and gait repeatedly changed with neurological treatment state. They do not establish that every foot symptom was centrally generated or that structural pathology was unreal.

The correct use of this evidence is to show why the foot/neurology relationship remained clinically informative and why later effects could reasonably prompt retrospective reconsideration.

Whether a levodopa trial should have been used earlier, and what clinical difference it would probably have made, are expert questions.

### 6. Date the patient's knowledge

The 19 March 2025 complaint is not the final theory. It expressly acknowledges incomplete access to internal material. It is powerful evidence of notice, bodily discovery and the patient's then-existing hypotheses.

By late May, FOI had materially changed his informational position. Later GDPR, governance and clinical material changed it again.

Never back-project the 2026 reconstruction into the 2017 patient or the March 2025 complainant.

### 7. Use the genetics as a constraint, not a retrospective trump card

The February 2026 result confirms PRKN/Parkin-related Parkinsonism and dystonia. It defeats both simplistic positions: that Parkinsonism was invented, and that the original Parkinson's label necessarily resolved the entire presentation.

It does not retrospectively determine breach, causation or the mechanism of every foot symptom.

## The central causation question

If the Protest is proved and its material omission established, causation must still be demonstrated.

The expert question is not merely whether a different sentence would have appeared in the discharge summary. It is whether accurate preservation of the unresolved foot/gait dispute would probably have changed the clinical sequence: further examination, movement-disorders review, delayed discharge, altered treatment, earlier genetic work-up, an explicit reopening plan, or later interpretation of medication effects.

That is where legal causation will stand or fall.

## Governing restraint

The strongest advocacy is achieved by refusing claims the evidence does not need.

Do not say:

- Parkinsonism was false;
- all foot pain was dystonia;
- Sinemet disproved Parkinson's disease;
- Podiatry was inherently wrong;
- all later care was containment;
- every governance process was circular;
- or the omission was deliberate unless evidence establishes purpose.

Say instead:

> **A real Parkinsonian disorder was diagnosed within a broader presentation whose foot/gait relationship remained unresolved. If the patient proved that he challenged that unresolved state before discharge, and that a material challenge known to the record-maker was omitted, the completed account may have transmitted a more settled information state than the encounter contained. The legal task is then to identify which later decisions depended materially on that state and what difference accurate recording and reconsideration would probably have made.**

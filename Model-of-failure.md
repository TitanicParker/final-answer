# A Model of Systems Failure

This model is not a finding that the system failed. It is a disciplined account of the mechanism that should be tested against the evidence.

## 1. A unified presentation enters the system

The patient presents with feet, gait, toe-clawing, tension, movement difficulty and declining function. The referral is unresolved: Parkinsonian or another underlying disorder.

## 2. One part of the presentation becomes highly legible

Neurology identifies a strong Parkinsonian syndrome. Examination, imaging, laboratory work, case discussion, treatment planning and genetic consideration all develop rapidly around that axis.

At the same time the feet remain clinically complicated: longstanding pain and Podiatry treatment, structural abnormalities, burning, possible peripheral explanations, dystonic-looking gait and an expressly unclear relationship to the neurological syndrome.

## 3. The integrative control question is not visibly resolved

The record contains the facts necessary to ask:

> **What are the painful feet doing to the gait, and what is the neurological disorder doing to the feet?**

The surviving reasoning does not visibly answer that reciprocal question before the case moves toward discharge.

## 4. Operational separation occurs

Parkinsonism becomes the dominant neurological frame. The feet move operationally toward Podiatry / peripheral explanation while the relationship remains uncertain.

Podiatry is not inherently inappropriate. The systems question is whether it functions as parallel management of a still-integrated problem or as a substitute for resolving the neurological relationship.

## 5. The patient says he objects

The patient later recalls protesting before discharge that the feet remain unresolved and using the NCT / bald-tyres analogy.

If established, this is an attempted contemporaneous correction of the emerging synthesis.

## 6. The durable account does not preserve the disagreement

The completed record preserves diagnosis, treatment rationale, patient understanding and Podiatry disposition, but not the reported Protest.

If the Protest occurred, was known and was material, the record may now carry a more settled information state than the encounter contained.

## 7. The record becomes the future's starting point

Later clinicians do not receive the original bedside state. They receive the durable record and its descendants.

That creates path dependence. New evidence can be absorbed into an existing model without automatically reconstructing the model's origin.

## 8. The body produces discordant information

Procyclidine benefit, withdrawal deterioration involving feet and gait, later dystonia/OFF-state discussion and levodopa-associated foot relief repeatedly demonstrate interaction between neurological treatment state and the feet.

This does not prove a single mechanism. It makes the foot/neurology relationship persistently informative.

## 9. The frame can alter the meaning of new evidence

If the feet are already classified as secondary, structural or peripheral, improvement in the feet with neurological medication can be interpreted as pain modulation rather than as evidence requiring reconstruction of the original relationship.

The potential systems failure is therefore interpretive rather than simply observational: **facts can be accurately recorded while their significance is constrained by the inherited frame.**

## 10. The patient lives the contradiction without the archive

For years the patient possesses symptoms, treatment effects and lived function. The institution possesses the durable representation and its provenance.

The asymmetry is not total secrecy. It is unequal control over how the case is represented and transmitted.

## 11. March 2025 breaks the equilibrium

Profound Sinemet CR relief makes the inherited explanation newly unstable to the patient. He complains before full access to internal records.

This is bodily discovery before documentary discovery.

## 12. Reconstruction begins

FOI, access requests, governance correspondence, data-rights processes and later clinical evidence progressively allow the patient to compare lived history with institutional history.

The information asymmetry narrows.

## 13. Governance can reproduce the same dependency problem

A later review may sincerely validate earlier care within the information available to it. But if those inputs descend materially from an earlier representation, the later conclusion does not independently prove that the originating representation was complete.

That is the risk of recursive validation.

## 14. Genetics closes one argument and opens a better one

PRKN genetics later confirms Parkinsonism and dystonia. The case can no longer responsibly be framed as "Parkinsonism versus dystonia."

The better question is whether the 2017 representation adequately integrated a disorder capable of producing both, particularly where the feet remained the persistent embodied and functional signal.

## The model in one line

> **Embodied signal -> incomplete integration -> attempted correction -> possible omission -> durable representation -> documentary dependency -> treatment-state contradictions -> bodily discovery -> archival reconstruction -> restoration of informational symmetry.**

Every arrow is a proposition to test. None should be assumed merely because the model is coherent.
